# Probability Playground — Teacher Pack & Classroom Worksheets

## Module Overview
**Target Level:** Key Stage 3–4 (KS3–KS4) Mathematics  
**Topic:** Theoretical vs. Experimental Probability, Law of Large Numbers, Central Limit Theorem  

---

## Lesson Plan Outline (60 Minutes)

### Phase 1: Starter & Theoretical Foundations (10 mins)
* Review probability scale $P(A) \in [0, 1]$.
* Discuss theoretical probability of single events:
  * Coin flip: $P(	ext{Heads}) = 0.5$
  * Fair 6-sided die: $P(X = k) = rac{1}{6}$ for $k \in \{1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6\}$

### Phase 2: Interactive Exploration using Probability Playground (25 mins)
1. **Coin Toss Experiment:**
   * Set $N = 10$, $N = 100$, $N = 1000$, and $N = 5000$.
   * Track cumulative relative frequency of heads.
   * Observe line convergence towards $0.50$.
2. **Sum of Two Dice (2d6):**
   * Predict the distribution shape of the sum of two dice.
   * Run $N = 1000$ rolls in Module 2.
   * Calculate why 7 is the modal outcome ($6/36 = 1/6$).

### Phase 3: Quiz & Discussion (15 mins)
* Complete the interactive quiz questions in Module 5.
* Discuss the Gambler's Fallacy and independence of trials.

### Phase 4: Reflection & Plenary (10 mins)
* Summarise how sample size impacts estimation accuracy (Law of Large Numbers).

## Student Worksheet & Problem Set

1. **Question 1 (Coin Tosses):**
   In a simulation of 500 coin flips, you record 272 heads.
   * (a) Calculate the experimental probability of landing on heads.
   * (b) Calculate the absolute difference from the theoretical probability ($0.5$).

2. **Question 2 (Two Dice Sums):**
   List all possible outcomes $(d_1, d_2)$ that yield a sum of $8$. Calculate $P(	ext{Sum} = 8)$.

3. **Question 3 (Concept Check):**
   Explain why increasing the number of trials in a Monte Carlo simulation provides a more reliable estimate of theoretical probability.
